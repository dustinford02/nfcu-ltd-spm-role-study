# Posting terms

These terms appear in the official posting. Every explanation below is **industry**, not an NFCU definition.

| Posting term | General explanation | Learning source |
|---|---|---|
| Performance management cycle | Connected expectations, feedback, development, evaluation, and recognition. | [OPM reference model](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/performance-management/performance-management-cycle/) |
| Performance calibration | Comparing performance judgments and evidence to improve consistency. Local rules and authority need confirmation. | [Oracle concept](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/human-resources/faiwc/calibrated-performance-ratings-in-a-workforce-compensation-plan.html) |
| Business case | Comparing an investment's options, costs, risks, and expected benefits against its strategic purpose. | [PMI benefits framework](https://www.pmi.org/learning/thought-leadership/series/benefits-realization/benefits-realization-management-framework) |
| Adoption and sustainment | Effective use of a change and continuation of the intended behavior after rollout. | [Prosci measurement](https://www.prosci.com/blog/metrics-for-measuring-change-management) |
| Power BI | Microsoft tools to connect, visualize, and share data; a product name alone says little about metric quality. | [Microsoft overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/power-bi-overview) |
| SAFe | A Lean-Agile framework with community guidance for people practices. Its mention does not establish an employer operating model. | [Scaled Agile HR guidance](https://framework.scaledagile.com/agile-hr) |

Talent Philosophy and Leadership Model are employer-named concepts whose content remains unestablished; they are not generic definitions to fill in.
