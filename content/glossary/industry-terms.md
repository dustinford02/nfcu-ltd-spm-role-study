# Industry concepts

Every definition and example is **industry**, not employer policy.

- **Calibration:** Discussion of performance judgments and their evidence to seek consistency. It does not automatically mean forced ranking. [Oracle concept](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/human-resources/faiwc/calibrated-performance-ratings-in-a-workforce-compensation-plan.html).
- **Performance-cycle stages:** OPM's reference links planning, monitoring, developing, rating, and rewarding. Its federal requirements are not transferred to this employer. [OPM cycle](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/performance-management/performance-management-cycle/).
- **Adoption:** Consider speed, breadth of use, and proficiency separately. [Prosci measurement](https://www.prosci.com/blog/metrics-for-measuring-change-management).
- **Benefits realization:** Connect intended value to strategy, deliver it, and sustain it after transition to operations. [PMI framework](https://www.pmi.org/learning/thought-leadership/series/benefits-realization/benefits-realization-management-framework).
- **RACI:** Responsible does the work; accountable owns completion or decision; consulted contributes expertise; informed receives updates. Validate actual authority. [Atlassian guidance](https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/project-management/raci-chart).
- **SAFe-in-HR caution:** The linked material is community guidance on people practices. It does not establish release trains, cadence, or software-style delivery requirements for this role. [Scaled Agile guidance](https://framework.scaledagile.com/agile-hr).
- **Leading versus lagging talent metrics:** Early indicators help assess progress before an intended outcome is observable; later measures check results over a defined period. Participation and sustained behavior are distinct illustrative measures, requiring a baseline, population, and attribution check. [Measurement basis](https://www.prosci.com/blog/metrics-for-measuring-change-management); [benefits basis](https://www.pmi.org/learning/thought-leadership/series/benefits-realization/benefits-realization-management-framework).
- **Output versus outcome:** A report or session is a deliverable; behavior change is an outcome; resulting organizational value is a benefit. Delivery does not establish all three. [PMI framework](https://www.pmi.org/learning/thought-leadership/series/benefits-realization/benefits-realization-management-framework).
