## Coverage limits

The study map prioritizes job duties, qualifications, overview, tools and role questions. Application accommodations, scam guidance, award names, EEO language and site-navigation text are preserved in source captures, not converted into candidate competencies. “Other duties as assigned” is retained as unspecified scope; no work is invented to expand it. BSA boilerplate is retained as a compliance obligation without inventing internal policy.

The saved PDF's overview and closing date differ from the official retrieval. The current page also carries degree wording that differs from its structured Degree Level field. Those tensions remain visible rather than silently reconciled.

## Candidate review

Ten bounded cards contain sixteen fact fragments and separate practice prompts. They distinguish supervision from mentorship, scope from achieved results, education from work, self-report from issuer-record copies, and a never-piloted personal concept from implemented experience. Specific held operational/contract material, original source documents, and private provenance paths are excluded.
