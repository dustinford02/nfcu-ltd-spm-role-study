# Oracle customer context

Oracle reports that Navy Federal uses Oracle Fusion Cloud Talent Management for goals, check-ins, and development, including embedded AI assistance.

The vendor reports **58% growth** in average performance goals per employee, an increase from **25,000 to 29,000** employees with performance goals, and **125% growth** in workers with development goals.

These remain **Oracle-reported measures**. The story does not identify this requisition as owner of the work, establish a causal evaluation, or supply a measurement period. Goal counts are not automatically workforce-performance gains.
