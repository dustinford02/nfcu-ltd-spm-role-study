# Bound the Oracle customer story

This is not an official interview question.

Class: prep_drill. Timebox: 3 minutes. Topics: public-employer-context, open-questions.

## Practice

Explain what the Oracle story establishes as vendor-reported context. Name three conclusions it cannot establish about this requisition.

## Self-check instructions

Do not infer ownership, privileges, implementation assignments, or hiring-manager identity from the story.
