# Risk, issue, or dependency?

This is not an official interview question.

Class: prep_drill. Timebox: 3 minutes. Topics: program-execution-governance.

## Practice

Classify three hypothetical statements: access might arrive late; access is overdue; training cannot begin until access works. Explain how the response differs.

## Self-check instructions

Keep uncertain risk, current issue, and sequencing dependency distinct. Allow a fact to link to multiple management records.

## Motivating posting excerpts
