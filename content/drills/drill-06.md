# Prepare a decision-focused update

This is not an official interview question.

Class: prep_drill. Timebox: 10 minutes. Topics: program-execution-governance.

## Practice

A hypothetical program is on schedule, but a critical decision has no owner. Give a 90-second senior update that makes the decision and consequences clear.

## Self-check instructions

Include status evidence, decision needed, options, an owner to confirm, and a date to agree; avoid activity counts without implications.
