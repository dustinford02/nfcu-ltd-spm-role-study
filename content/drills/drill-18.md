# Read qualification levels precisely

This is not an official interview question.

Class: prep_drill. Timebox: 10 minutes. Topics: qualifications-methods-tools.

## Practice

Create columns for experience requirements, knowledge/skill expectations, and desired qualifications. Place each sampled phrase correctly.

## Self-check instructions

Preserve five-plus versus two-plus years, equivalency wording, and desired status. Flag conflicting degree metadata without guessing its resolution.
