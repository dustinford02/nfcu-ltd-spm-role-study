# Audit a claim of success

This is not an official interview question.

Class: prep_drill. Timebox: 25 minutes. Topics: investment-business-case-outcomes, change-adoption-mentoring.

## Practice

A hypothetical report says all managers completed training, so the change succeeded. Identify missing evidence and design a proportionate follow-up evaluation.

## Self-check instructions

Separate attendance, understanding, actual use, proficiency, and sustained results. State what evidence would change the conclusion.
