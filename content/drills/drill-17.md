# Choose a delivery approach conditionally

This is not an official interview question.

Class: prep_drill. Timebox: 25 minutes. Topics: qualifications-methods-tools, program-execution-governance.

## Practice

A hypothetical annual cycle has a fixed date but uncertain reporting requirements. Propose a delivery approach and identify work suited to iteration versus explicit sequencing.

## Self-check instructions

Justify choices from constraints. Do not assume SAFe adoption, release trains, or a prescribed NFCU methodology.
