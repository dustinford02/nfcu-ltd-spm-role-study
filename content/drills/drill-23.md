# Design a discovery workshop

This is not an official interview question.

Class: prep_drill. Timebox: 25 minutes. Topics: overview-vs-duties-tension, open-questions, investment-business-case-outcomes.

## Practice

Plan a hypothetical workshop to understand a talent-program portfolio. Produce an agenda, needed inputs, decision questions, and outputs.

## Self-check instructions

Invent no current problems. Separate verified scope, stakeholder hypotheses, success measures, constraints, and unresolved decisions.
