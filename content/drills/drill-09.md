# Compare investment options

This is not an official interview question.

Class: prep_drill. Timebox: 25 minutes. Topics: investment-business-case-outcomes.

## Practice

A hypothetical sponsor can improve manager guidance, automate reminders, or redesign reporting. Compare options using assumptions, costs, benefits, risks, and adoption effort.

## Self-check instructions

Invent no employer budget. Identify needed data, sensitivity to assumptions, and a recommendation conditional on that data.
