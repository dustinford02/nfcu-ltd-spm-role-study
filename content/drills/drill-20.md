# Interpret a vendor metric carefully

This is not an official interview question.

Class: prep_drill. Timebox: 10 minutes. Topics: public-employer-context, investment-business-case-outcomes.

## Practice

Oracle reports growth in goals and goal participation. Explain what further evidence would be needed to conclude workforce performance improved.

## Self-check instructions

Preserve vendor attribution. Distinguish activity volume, coverage, quality, and outcomes; request period and measurement design.
