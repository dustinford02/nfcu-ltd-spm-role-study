# Mentor a lead through a dependency

This is not an official interview question.

Class: prep_drill. Timebox: 10 minutes. Topics: change-adoption-mentoring, program-execution-governance.

## Practice

A hypothetical team lead escalates every dependency as an emergency. Conduct a short coaching exchange that develops judgment instead of taking over decisions.

## Self-check instructions

Ask for impact and evidence, agree escalation criteria, let the lead choose a next action, and define a reflection point.
