# Build a balanced metric pair

This is not an official interview question.

Class: prep_drill. Timebox: 10 minutes. Topics: investment-business-case-outcomes, performance-cycle-calibration.

## Practice

Design one early indicator and one later outcome for a hypothetical cycle improvement. Define numerator, denominator, data owner, and reporting cadence.

## Self-check instructions

Do not present these as NFCU metrics. Explain how the pair could mislead and what cross-check would help.
