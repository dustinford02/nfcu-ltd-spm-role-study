# Outputs, outcomes, and benefits

This is not an official interview question.

Class: prep_drill. Timebox: 10 minutes. Topics: investment-business-case-outcomes.

## Practice

For a hypothetical manager-support program, propose one deliverable, one behavior change, and one organizational benefit. Explain evidence that distinguishes them.

## Self-check instructions

State population, baseline, period, and attribution limits; a completed launch is not automatically an achieved workforce outcome.

## Motivating posting excerpts

> Proven ability to align program execution with organizational goals, manage stakeholder expectations, and drive measurable outcomes
