# Map authority before assigning it

This is not an official interview question.

Class: prep_drill. Timebox: 10 minutes. Topics: program-execution-governance, open-questions.

## Practice

Sketch a provisional responsibility map for HR, finance, operations, technology, and the program manager. Mark every assignment needing employer confirmation.

## Self-check instructions

This is a proposed map, not an org chart. Identify who must validate actual decision rights.
